Privacy Policy

for wq-agent

Last updated: March 23, 2026

Overview

wq-agent is a personal productivity tool used by Qing Wang to read, create, update, and delete Google Calendar events for scheduling and calendar management.

Information Accessed

The application may access Google Calendar account data that is necessary to provide calendar-related functionality, including:

  • Calendar event titles, times, descriptions, and related scheduling metadata
  • Calendar availability information used to check conflicts and manage events

How Information Is Used

The accessed Google Calendar data is used solely to support personal scheduling tasks, including viewing events, checking availability, and creating, updating, or deleting calendar entries requested by the account owner.

Data Sharing

Google Calendar data accessed by wq-agent is not sold or shared with third parties except as required to operate the Google Calendar API or to comply with applicable law.

Data Retention and Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored only for maintaining access to the authorized Google Calendar account. Reasonable efforts are made to keep these credentials private and restricted to the authorized user environment.

Revoking Access

You can revoke the application's access at any time from your Google Account permissions settings.
